## What changed

CFPB ANPR responding to an August 2024 NCLC/CSAJ petition: the CFPB states it has determined a rulemaking is warranted and will propose amending the Regulation V definitions of 'identity theft' and 'identity theft report' (under 15 U.S.C. 1681a(q)(3)) to cover coerced debt, i.e., transactions without the consumer's effective consent, so survivors could use identity theft blocks under 15 U.S.C. 1681c-2. It asks seven sets of questions, including what documentation or self-attestation should prove coercion and whether presumptions should apply. Comments due March 7, 2025 (extended to April 7, 2025 by 2025-03685).
