## What changed

NCUA final rule, effective September 8, 2026, adopting its March 2026 proposal without change after 14 supportive comments: removes 12 CFR 701.21(h) (limits on federal credit union purchases of indirect vehicle loans serviced by any one third-party servicer to 50 percent of net worth, 100 percent after 30 months, with waivers), removes 741.203(c) (same limits for federally insured state-chartered credit unions) and removes the 701.21(h)(3) citation from 746.201(c). The preamble says boards remain responsible for policies scaled to their indirect lending and that NCUA vendor-management, enterprise-risk and governance expectations continue to apply through examination.

No eCFR diff: the document does not amend a tracked CFR part.

## What a compliant agent must now do

From September 8, 2026, federally insured credit unions are no longer subject to a regulatory cap on indirect vehicle loans serviced by a single third-party servicer. A servicing provider's agent behavior does not change, but credit union clients' due diligence and ongoing monitoring of the servicer (under their own board policies and NCUA examination) become the controlling oversight.
