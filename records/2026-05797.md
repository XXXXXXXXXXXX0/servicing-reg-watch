## What changed

NCUA proposes removing 12 CFR 701.21(h) (third-party servicing of indirect vehicle loans: a federal credit union may purchase indirect vehicle loans and participations serviced by any one third-party servicer only up to 50 percent of net worth, rising to 100 percent after 30 months with that servicer, with a waiver process in (h)(2)-(3)), removing 741.203(c) (applying the same limits to federally insured state-chartered credit unions) and conforming 746.201(c). Credit union boards would set their own policies; NCUA would continue to review these purchases in examinations. Comments due May 26, 2026.
