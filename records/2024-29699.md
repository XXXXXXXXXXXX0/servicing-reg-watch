## What changed

CFPB final rule (89 FR 106768; effective October 1, 2025 as published) amending Regulations E and Z so that overdraft credit from very large financial institutions (over $10 billion in assets) is consumer credit under Regulation Z unless the fee only recovers estimated costs and losses (new 12 CFR 1026.62; changes to 1026.1-1026.4, 1026.42, 1026.60, the Regulation Z commentary, and 1005.10(e)(1) and its commentary). Above-breakeven overdraft credit accessed by a hybrid debit-credit card is brought under the credit card provisions, including the cardholder's right to assert claims and defenses against the card issuer (1026.12(c)) and billing-error resolution (1026.13). The committed eCFR store shows 12 CFR 1026.62 text in effect on 2025-10-01; no later document in the store disapproves or removes the rule.

No eCFR diff: the document does not amend a tracked CFR part.
