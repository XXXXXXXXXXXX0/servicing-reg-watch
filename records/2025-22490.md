## What changed

NCUA proposes removing Appendix B to 12 CFR part 748 (Guidance on Response Programs for Unauthorized Access to Member Information and Member Notice, issued June 2005) from the CFR and publishing the same content as guidance in a Letter to Credit Unions. NCUA says Appendix B is guidance for the response programs its regulation requires, that its CFR placement may confuse credit unions about its nonbinding status, and that the change alters no substantive compliance obligation. Comments were due February 9, 2026. The companion proposal 2025-22489 does the same for Appendix A (Guidelines for Safeguarding Member Information).
