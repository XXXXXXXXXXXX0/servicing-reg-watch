## What changed

CFPB advisory opinion interpreting FDCPA 807(2)(A)/808(1) and 12 CFR 1006.18(b)(2)(i) and 1006.22(b) for medical debt. Part III.A states that a debt collector may collect only the amount actually owed at each collection attempt after deducting partial payments by the consumer or third parties, and that collecting the original amount after a partial payment is both unfair (1006.22(b)) and a misrepresentation of the amount (1006.18(b)(2)(i)). Part III.B: amounts not owed under federal or state law are not 'permitted by law'. The opinion also says collectors need a reasonable basis to substantiate amounts, treats 1692e(2)(A) and 1692f(1) as strict liability, and interprets 'in default' under 803(6)(F)(iii) for medical debt. Applicable December 3, 2024 (moved to January 2, 2025 by 2024-27791). Withdrawn May 12, 2025 in 2025-08286 (item 3 of that notice).
