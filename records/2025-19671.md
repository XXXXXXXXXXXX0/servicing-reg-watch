## What changed

CFPB interpretive rule (applicable October 28, 2025) confirming withdrawal of its July 2022 FCRA preemption interpretive rule (87 FR 41042) and stating that 15 U.S.C. 1681t(b)(1) broadly preempts state law: under 1681t(b)(1)(F), 'any State law that concerns the responsibilities of furnishers' (the subject matter of 1681s-2) is preempted, and under 1681t(b)(1)(E) all state laws on the inclusion of information in consumer reports (e.g., medical debt, rental information, arrest records, which the 2022 rule had said states could regulate) are preempted. The rule acknowledges agencies have no special authority to pronounce on preemption, that courts decide, and that the First, Second and Ninth Circuits (Frey, Galper, Aargon) have read the clause narrowly.
