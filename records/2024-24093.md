## What changed

CFPB Supervisory Highlights Issue 35 (auto finance; exams completed Nov. 1, 2023 to Aug. 30, 2024) reports UDAAP findings under 12 U.S.C. 5531 against auto servicers: sec. 2.2.1 wrongful repossession when repossession orders were not cancelled after payments, extensions, deferments or modifications or payment arrangements sufficient to stop repossession; sec. 2.2.2 repossessing vehicles without a recorded lien; sec. 2.3.1 applying post-maturity payments in an order different from the order disclosed on the website, producing late fees (deceptive and unfair); sec. 2.3.2 excessive delay delivering titles after payoff or for out-of-state registration transfer, against a two-business-day policy; sec. 2.4.1 collecting add-on product charges (service contracts, GAP) consumers did not agree to in refinances marketed by service providers (abusive), with direction to monitor consumer-facing service providers and record all calls; sec. 2.4.2 financing GAP that was void on salvage-title vehicles; sec. 2.4.4 requiring two in-person dealership visits to cancel add-on products (abusive). Origination findings (sec. 2.1, 2.4.3: 'as low as' APR marketing, Reg Z 1026.17(c)(1), 1026.18(c)(1)(iii)) are outside servicing. The introduction also cites incorrect payment-history furnishing, in a part not read.

## What a compliant agent must now do

> Change type is `interpretation`: validate existing controls against the text below. Change agent behavior only where validation finds a gap.

Auto servicers (first-party lenders and servicers acting for them), as UDAAP supervisory expectations: (1) before and during any repossession workflow, check for payments, promises to pay, extensions, deferrals or modifications that should stop it, and cancel or reverse the repossession order immediately; confirm a recorded lien before assigning any vehicle for repossession; (2) apply payments, including on post-maturity loans, in exactly the order disclosed to borrowers, and never quote a different order; (3) when a loan is paid off or a borrower requests title for re-registration, trigger title/lien release within the servicer's stated timeline and tell the borrower the true timeline; (4) do not add, retain or collect for add-on products the borrower did not affirmatively agree to, explain voluntariness and cost in any refinance conversation, and let borrowers cancel add-ons without unreasonable steps (no in-person-only cancellation); ensure refunds of unearned add-on premiums on early payoff or default; (5) lenders deploying an agent must monitor it as a consumer-facing service provider, including recording and reviewing calls.
