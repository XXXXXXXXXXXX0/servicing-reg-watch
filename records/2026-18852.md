## What changed

Federal Reserve proposes a Third-Party Risk Management Guide for Traditional Community Banking Organizations (under $30 billion in assets, not complex bank-fintech partnerships), a companion to the proposed all-bank TPRM guidance (2026-18859). Sec. IV.B covers operational resilience, system and information security, compliance with rules and regulations, and financial resilience of vendors; sec. IV.C gives vendor-by-vendor considerations for core, IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, payment processing and digital banking, loan management system, card, BSA/AML and fraud providers. The guide states it is not a rule, banks are not required to take the actions described, and consumer compliance is out of scope. Comments due November 16, 2026.

No eCFR diff: the document does not amend a tracked CFR part.
