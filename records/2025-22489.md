## What changed

NCUA proposes removing Appendix A to 12 CFR part 748 (Guidelines for Safeguarding Member Information) from the CFR and providing its content through a Letter to Credit Unions. NCUA says Appendix A was issued to meet its statutory duty under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act to set standards for federally insured credit unions to protect the security and confidentiality of member information, and that the proposal removes nonbinding guidelines but would retain them in another format without substantive change. Comments were due February 9, 2026 (docket NCUA-2025-1304). The companion proposal 2025-22490 does the same for Appendix B (response programs and member notice).
