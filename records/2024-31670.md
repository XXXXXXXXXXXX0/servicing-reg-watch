## What changed

CFPB Supervisory Highlights Issue 37 (exams Jan. 1 to Oct. 1, 2024). Furnishing (sec. 2.2): installment loan furnishers had no procedures to respond to CRC identity-theft block notifications and refurnished blocked information (15 U.S.C. 1681s-2(a)(6)(A)); debt collector furnishers verified indirect disputes through automated systems that checked only their own records, without their clients' records, and agents forwarded disputes to clients and told CRCs to delete tradelines when clients did not respond (1681s-2(b)(1)); student loan and installment loan furnishers lacked reasonable written accuracy and integrity policies (12 CFR 1022.42(a)-(b)), leading to reporting of bankruptcy-discharged accounts, wrong special comment codes, ignored CRC rejection feedback and mismatched consumer data. Payments (sec. 2.1.3): ODFIs failed to monitor originator clients re-presenting returned ACH debits as new entries (unfair), noting network rules cap re-presentments at two after an NSF return; sec. 2.1.4: debit card network operators processed recurring debits after valid stop-payment orders (Reg E 1005.10(c), unfair). Deposit overdraft/NSF findings (2.1.1, 2.1.2) are outside scope. BNPL and paycheck-advance findings are in the unread remainder.

## What a compliant agent must now do

> Change type is `interpretation`: validate existing controls against the text below. Change agent behavior only where validation finds a gap.

Lenders and servicers that furnish, and collectors acting for them: (1) maintain and follow procedures to act on CRC identity-theft block notifications and never refurnish blocked information; (2) investigate every indirect dispute against all records reasonably available, including the creditor client's system of record, rather than automated self-verification or deletion by default; (3) keep written accuracy and integrity policies (1022.42) covering bankruptcy discharges, comment codes and CRC rejection feedback. Lenders collecting by ACH: when a debit is returned for insufficient funds, re-present it only as a properly coded re-presentment, no more than the network limit, and never as a new authorization; honor borrower stop-payment requests on recurring debits.
