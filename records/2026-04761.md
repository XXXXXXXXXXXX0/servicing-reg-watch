## What changed

NCUA proposes to rewrite 12 CFR part 749 as a vital records preservation program, update its definitions, add an expectation that a credit union oversee any third-party service provider maintaining its vital records, and remove Appendix A (Record Retention Guidelines, suggested retention periods for credit union records) and Appendix B (Catastrophic Act Preparedness Guidelines) from the CFR. NCUA states the appendices were nonbinding but were followed as requirements; other records preservation requirements are not superseded.
