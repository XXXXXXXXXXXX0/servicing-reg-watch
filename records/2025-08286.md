## What changed

CFPB withdraws 67 guidance documents as of May 12, 2025 (sec. III), stating the guidance 'should not be enforced or otherwise relied upon by the Bureau' while it reviews them, that withdrawal is not necessarily final, and that it will deprioritize enforcement against conduct that does not conform to the guidance. Withdrawn items that bear on consumer-loan servicing include: advisory opinions on Regulation F pay-to-pay fees (87 FR 39733), Regulation F time-barred debt (88 FR 26475), Regulation F medical debt (89 FR 80715), FCRA permissible purposes (87 FR 41243), FCRA name-only matching (86 FR 62468), and Regulation B revocations or unfavorable changes to existing credit (87 FR 30097); the Policy Statement on abusive acts or practices (88 FR 21883); interpretive rules on FCRA preemption (87 FR 41042), state enforcement of the CFPA (87 FR 31940) and exams for servicemember risks (86 FR 32723); Circulars 2022-04 (data security), 2022-07 (reasonable investigation of disputes) and 2024-03 (unenforceable contract terms); Bulletins 2022-04 (mitigating harm from auto repossession), 2023-01 (collection after bankruptcy discharge of certain student loans), 2013-07 (UDAAP in collection of consumer debts), 2014-01 and 2013-09 (furnisher investigations and 'all relevant' information), 2015-07 (in-person collection) and 2013-01 (indirect auto lending under ECOA).
